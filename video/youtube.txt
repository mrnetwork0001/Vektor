TITLE
Vektor: the quant terminal that pays its builder on every fill | Strike Finance hackathon

DESCRIPTION
Vektor is a quant terminal for Strike Finance perps. It runs a grid bot, a funding harvester, the Aegis liquidation shield and server-run Autopilot, and the Vektor builder code rides on every order it places.
